03 — Under the hood

Built on free, real APIs.
No smoke and mirrors.

Text — Google Gemini

Captions, hashtags, blog posts, scripts, story/movie/content ideas, and image-prompt writing all run on Gemini's free tier (gemini-1.5-flash). Grab a free key at aistudio.google.com.

Images — Pollinations.ai

Gemini expands your idea into a rich prompt, then Pollinations' free, keyless endpoint renders it into an image — no billing, no signup.

Video & music — concept briefs

No free API renders actual video or audio yet, so Zenith AI has Gemini write a full director/producer-ready concept instead. Swap in a paid model later — the code's ready for it.
